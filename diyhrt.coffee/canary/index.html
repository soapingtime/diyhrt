warrant canary

a regularly re-signed PGP message saying the person running diyhrt.coffee is alive, free, in full control of the site, and hasn't been served any warrants, gag orders, or orders to change it. anyone can check the signature with gpg.

it works because of what it can't say. a gag order would stop us announcing it, but we could stop re-signing. a missing or stale signature is the alarm.

// check it yourself
  1. 1

    save the key and the message

    use the save buttons above to get diyhrt.asc and canary.txt exactly as published.

  2. 2

    import the key

    $ gpg --import diyhrt.asc
  3. 3

    check it's the right key

    $ gpg --fingerprint 33AA9E20C6D5FA57B056C488E608EDEDD5FB9ECF

    the fingerprint it prints should match the one above. this is the step that ties the key to us; to rule out a hijacked site swapping keys, compare it with an archived copy of this page (web.archive.org).

  4. 4

    verify the message

    $ gpg --verify canary.txt

    look for Good signature. gpg will also warn the key isn't certified; that's normal for a key you haven't signed yourself, and it's why step 3 matters. a good signature proves who signed it, not that the words are true.
