// worth knowing
  • the line is the model's best guess for an average person. the uncertainty dots show how unsure the model's own parameters are, which estrannai.se notes understates how different real people are. for patches, possibly by ~10×.
  • steady state (dashed) is where a repeating dose settles once you've been on it a while. the solid line shows the climb there from your first dose.
  • the target band defaults to 100–200 pg/mL, the range estrannai.se draws from WPATH SOC 8 and the Endocrine Society guideline. you can change it under bloodwork.
  • calibration scales every curve. 1.0 is the model as published. if your bloodwork keeps landing above or below the line, “fit to my bloodwork” finds the scale that matches it best.
  • the menstrual cycle overlay is an average cycle (Stricker et al. 2006), median with 5th–95th percentiles, for context.
