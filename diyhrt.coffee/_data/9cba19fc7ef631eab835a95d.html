  2. update

    heads up: raiders

    5d ago

    hi everyone, this one isn't a fun update. a group from soyjak.st has started a "raid" campaign against DIY HRT. they've named a bunch of sites and vendors, including this one, and they're openly talking about trying to find out who runs them, getting alt accounts into our discord to collect what people post, and trying to get sites taken down. what's going on on our end: - the site is fine. there's no sign of any breach and nothing about any of you has been posted anywhere - we've tightened a lot of things up behind the scenes - the discord is locked down for now while we sort things out. announcements and stock alerts are still there and alerts go out like normal, the chat channels are hidden until it's over - general chat was cleared and will reopen once things calm down what you can do: - don't share anything that could identify you, on the site, on discord, or anywhere else - be careful with DMs from people you don't know, especially new accounts asking where you live, what you order or who runs things - don't click random links - don't engage with them or go into their threads. attention is exactly what they want - if you see anything about the site or its members posted somewhere, send it to me through the contact form or simplex, and please don't repost it the stock tracker, alerts and everything else keep running as normal. stay safe and look out for each other ♡ - Akira

  3. feature

    diyhrt 2.0

    6d ago

    hiii so this one's a big one, we're calling it 2.0 pretty much every page got redone. home, catalog, availability, vendors, stats, uptime, reviews, verified, canary, the start guide, faq, resources, settings, all of it. same look you're used to, just cleaner and a lot easier to read, with proper charts instead of walls of numbers. the catalog sorts things by what they actually are now (valerate, enanthate, cypionate, gels, progesterone etc) instead of whatever each shop decided to call it, and the in stock section on the home page actually shows you what's in stock at a glance the e2 calculator got rebuilt from scratch. you can stack as many injections/patches/whatever as you want, tweak each one, and save your regimens to slots so you don't have to punch everything back in every time. the model is the same one estrannai.se uses so the numbers line up with theirs new stuff: - rss feed for restocks, grab the link from the bottom of settings. no account needed and you can filter it by region or shop - PawSnax is on the tracker (US only). they're tagged returning since they're not exactly new to this - reviews page is completely redone, way easier to find what people actually said about a shop fixes: - a vendor shutting down or getting suspended doesn't count as downtime on the uptime page anymore - bunch of smaller layout stuff if something looks broken or weird after the update, tell me through the contact form or the discord and i'll fix it stay safe! - Akira
