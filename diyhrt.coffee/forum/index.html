// the rules, short version5 of them

  1. 1be kind

    everyone here is somewhere different with this. ask before you correct someone, and assume they mean well until they prove otherwise.

  2. 2stay anonymous

    no real names, faces, addresses, workplaces or anything else that ties an account to a person, yours or anyone else's. we quietly remove it when we see it.

  3. 3not medical advice

    people share what worked for them. that's harm reduction, not a prescription. when in doubt: bloodwork, and a pharmacist if you can reach one.

  4. 4no selling or trading

    talking about vendors, lab results and experiences is the point. selling, swapping or brokering meds isn't, in threads or in dms.

  5. 5report, don't pile on

    if something's off, hit report and let a mod handle it. warnings add up, and enough of them turn into a temporary ban.
