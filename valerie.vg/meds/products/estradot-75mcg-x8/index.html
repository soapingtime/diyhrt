Estradot is a matrix-type transdermal patch that provides a slow and steady release of bioidentical Estradiol into the body through the skin. This is the 75mcg dose variant.

These patches should be worn for 4 days.

Estradot is the brand name for Novartis's Estradiol patches and can be safely mixed and matched with any other brand of 75mcg Estradiol patches.
